Description

Identified non-conformity in the safety procedures during the last audit. The emergency exits were blocked and fire extinguishers were not properly maintained.

Root Cause

Lack of regular safety inspections and inadequate staff training on emergency procedures.

Initial Action Plan

Schedule immediate safety training for all staff and implement a weekly inspection checklist for emergency equipment and exits.
